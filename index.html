The Iceberg

It doesn't just keep your ideas. It gives them back.

The line in your notes app. The melody you hummed into your phone. The chord you'll forget by morning. The Iceberg catches them in seconds. New ideas float near the surface and older ones sink deeper, but they never vanish: one day it brings an old one back up to you, right when you need a spark.

  • Nothing gets lost

    "Break the Ice" lifts a forgotten idea back to the surface. Your old work keeps coming back to you.

  • Catch it in seconds

    Write a line or hum a melody straight from the home screen. Capture comes first, always.

  • An iceberg of ideas

    Every idea is a small light in the ice. Turn it, tilt it, look down from above. It grows deeper as your ideas do.

  • Gentle on tired days

    "I have five minutes" offers one small thing from your own ideas. No streaks, no guilt.

  • Sketch the song

    Chords, drums, a little keyboard, a tuner and a metronome, for turning a fragment into a song.

  • Yours alone

    Everything stays on your phone. No account, no cloud, no ads. Your words are never rewritten.

Little secrets surface in the deep as your iceberg grows: a fish, a star, a passing whale…

Why I'm making it

Most songs don't disappear because they're bad. They disappear because we're tired, or busy, or a little lazy that day, and the idea slips into a notes app nobody opens again.

I wanted a place that catches those pieces without asking anything back, keeps them safe, and then does the part nobody else does: brings them back to you. The half-song from last spring, the line from a sleepless night. Nothing you made should be lost just because you were tired.

That's The Iceberg. Small, private and made with care.
