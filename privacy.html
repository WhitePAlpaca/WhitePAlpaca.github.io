Privacy

Effective date: 1 December 2026 · App: The Iceberg (Android, package com.theiceberg.app)

The short version

The Iceberg does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no internet access. Everything you create stays on your phone.

What the app stores, and where

Everything below is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. The developer never receives it.

WhatWhy
Ideas you write (lyrics, titles, notes, chords, key, BPM)So you can keep and organise them
Voice memos you record or share into the appSo you can play them back
Projects, links between ideas, Drift (items you set aside)So you can develop songs
Settings (theme, language, whether you've seen the welcome screen)So the app remembers your choices
A short local history of which ideas "Break the Ice" showed youSo it doesn't show the same idea twice in a row
Studio work in progress (beats, melodies, song sketches, unsaved lyric sheets)So nothing is lost if you leave a screen
Writing room choices (scene, sound, focus) and your measured vocal range (two notes)So the tools remember how you like them

Permissions

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) — used only while you are actively recording a voice memo you started, or while the tuner or vocal tools are open on screen. Recordings are saved to the app's private storage. In the tuner and vocal tools the sound is analysed in memory for pitch and immediately discarded; nothing is recorded. Audio is never uploaded, streamed or analysed off-device.
  • The app does not request internet access, location, contacts, camera, storage-wide access or any other permission.

Backups

The app never backs itself up to any cloud on its own (Android's automatic cloud backup is switched off for The Iceberg). If you choose "Save a backup", the app writes a ZIP file to the location you pick (for example your Downloads folder or Google Drive through Android's file picker). That file is under your control; the developer never sees it.

If you turn on Automatic backups, you choose a folder once (through Android's folder picker) and the app writes a new backup file there about once a week, keeping the newest three of its own files. If that folder is synced by another app (for example Google Drive), that app uploads it under its own terms; The Iceberg itself still has no internet access.

Optional "Melody Sketch" (experimental)

If you turn on Melody Sketch, the app analyses a voice memo on your phone to estimate musical notes. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Crash reports

The app contains no crash-reporting or tracking SDK. If you install it from Google Play, Google may collect anonymous crash and performance statistics ("Android vitals") under Google's privacy policy, depending on your device settings. The developer only sees aggregated statistics, never your content.

Children

The Iceberg is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete items inside the app (Drift → Delete forever), or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored. Backup files you saved yourself must be deleted by you.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new effective date. A version that collects data would require your consent first.

Contact

Questions: use the contact email shown on The Iceberg's Google Play listing.
